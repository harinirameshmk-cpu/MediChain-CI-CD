MediChain Frontend + Customer/Admin Update

1. Added Customer as a real account role.
2. Customer registration and login are supported.
3. Customer dashboard is separate and read-only.
4. Customer can search available medicines by medicine name or Batch ID.
5. Customer can click Verify to check the batch against the blockchain/hash chain.
6. Customer dashboard shows medicine details, supply-chain stages, temperature/expiry status, and cryptographic block hashes.
7. Admin registration remains supported as a separate account role.
8. Login/Register pages now contain Customer and Admin roles with cleaner text labels.
9. Frontend styling was refined to a simple, elegant, consistent system font and cleaner spacing, borders, buttons, cards, and tables.
10. Fixed backend route imports so Express mounts routes only after MongoDB is connected.
11. Customer API routes are protected by authentication and customer/admin role checks.
