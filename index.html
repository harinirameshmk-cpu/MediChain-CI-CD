Why MediChain?

MediChain provides transparent and traceable medicine movement using blockchain technology. Every major supply-chain event is recorded as a tamper-evident transaction, allowing pharmacies and customers to verify the medicine's history, ensure safe refrigeration, and detect counterfeiting before consumption.
